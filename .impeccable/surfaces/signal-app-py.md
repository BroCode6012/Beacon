## Scope and mode

Full Signal desktop application redesign. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

A private, non-technical solo operator scans all five screens daily to identify
faceless, replicable YouTube opportunities, understand why they qualify, and
monitor the systems collecting and organizing them.

## Tasks and evidence

Preserve every existing workflow. Make channel comparison, filter state,
discovery freshness, research coverage, pipeline activity, and agent actions
immediately scannable. Real index data and existing system status are the only
proof; do not add invented claims.

## Constraints

Flet desktop implementation, one operator, no team UI, no direct SQLite access,
and no changes to the shared engine. Keep all five screens equally resolved.
Avoid excessive darkness, playfulness, decorative dashboards, and copied
Nexlev branding.

## Direction and memorable moment

Daylight Flight Deck: a bright operational research desk with an ink-blue rail,
cool paper surfaces, compact outlined controls, and signal-green state marks.
Nexlev supplies the readability and creator-tool density; Signal differentiates
through evidence strips, operational state, and replicability-first hierarchy.
The memorable moment is a channel result reading like a compact opportunity
brief: identity, eligibility, proof metrics, and confidence in one sweep.
